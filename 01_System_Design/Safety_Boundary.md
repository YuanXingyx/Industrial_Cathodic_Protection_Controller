# Safety Boundary

## Allowed for V0/V1

USB and 3.3 V, 5 V, 12 V, or at most 24 V DC low-voltage experiments.

## Prohibited implementation

220/380 VAC, mains rectification, high-voltage DC buses, high-power IGBT/SCR stages, and high-voltage isolated gate drives. The repository may describe a high-level industrial interface only as Future Work; it shall not supply an implementation for those circuits in V0/V1.

Before energizing any experiment, record supply voltage, current limit, wiring review, hardware revision and instrument setup.
